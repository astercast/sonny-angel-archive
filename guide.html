Real or fake.

Six checks. A real figure passes all six; a fake usually fails two or three.

First, the website check

Fake sites copy the official store's name, photos and descriptions. The one official USA and Canada store is sonnyangelusa.com. Read the address bar letter by letter before you pay.

  1. 1 Packaging

    Crisp print, true colors, clean folds, the Dreams copyright line on the back. Inside, a real figure sits in an opaque branded foil bag; a clear or unbranded bag means it is almost certainly fake. Flimsy card, blurry art or misspelled words give it away.

  2. 2 Print on the figure

    Eyes level and centered. Lashes and brows fine and even. The mouth small and clean. Smudged lines or colors bleeding past their edge are counterfeit signs.

  3. 3 Paint and finish

    Matte, even, vibrant, with clean edges between colors. No gloss patches, no bubbles, no rough seams, no sharp flashing when you run a thumb over the headgear.

  4. 4 Weight and feel

    Solid and dense for its size, about 3 inches tall. Official listings put mini figures around 25 to 34 g depending on the series. Light, hollow or wobbly on its feet is a warning sign.

  5. 5 Markings

    Look at the feet. One foot reads Sonny Angel with the Dreams copyright and the mould year; the other reads Made in China. Blank, misspelled or missing markings are the clearest fake tell there is.

  6. 6 Price

    Standard boxes run 12.25 to 13.75 dollars at the official store, HIPPERS 13.75. Far under that is the classic counterfeit pattern. Far over it is gouging, real or fake.

If you already bought one

Photograph the box and the figure in daylight. Compare with the photo on its row in the archive. If the checks say fake, contact the seller and your card issuer promptly.
