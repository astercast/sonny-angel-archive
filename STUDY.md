# STUDY, Sonny Angel USA Archive (Site 11)

Filed before code, per the fleet design doctrine. Order of
record: the user's direct order of 2026-10-14, a simple and
perfect easy to navigate site, inspired by Apple.com, of all
Sonny Angel USA releases ever in chronological order, with
eBay listings, fake warnings, a how to tell if it is fake
guide, pictures for each drop, and a restock watchlist of
verified stores only. His bar: best Sonny Angel USA website
ever.

## Who it is for

A collector standing in a store aisle, phone in one hand,
holding a box, asking three questions in this order: what
series is this, is it real, and what is a fair way to get it.
Every design choice answers those three questions fast.

## Three directions weighed

**A. The Chronological River (chosen).** One page that is
the archive: a ruled river of drops running 2004 to now,
year markers set large in a warm serif, each drop one row
with its date, its picture or its designed absence plate,
its lineup, and one quiet outbound eBay search link. Search
sits in the chrome and filters the river in place. Two
further pages only: the fake guide and the watchlist.
Reasons: this is the user's Apple direction at its purest,
the contents page as the whole product. For a catalog, the
index is the brand (fleet FINDINGS principle 10). It is
also the only direction that scales to hundreds of drops
without redesigning.

**B. The Boutique Shelf.** Drops as photographed objects on
soft shelves, one screen per series, heavy imagery, slow
scroll storytelling. Refused: it performs cute instead of
being useful, it breaks the three questions above, and it
collapses the moment half the drops have no verified photo,
which is the honest state of the catalog today.

**C. The Resale Board.** Prices and listings first, a market
ticker surface. Refused outright: the user's rule is the
Marvel rule, outbound links only, no on site buying or
selling, and a price board would put the archive in the
resale business and age in a day.

## Design system

- **Type.** Two voices, no webfonts. A warm serif (Georgia)
  for drop names, year markers and headlines. The system
  sans for dates, data and UI. Micro type, tracked capitals,
  for labels only. Nothing in the middle sizes.
- **Palette.** Warm paper ground #FBF7EE. Ink #2B2118, a
  warm brown black. One accent, cherry #C9331F, with a job:
  it marks dates, secrets, and warnings, and nothing else.
  No gradients, no glow, no glass.
- **Spacing.** Contents page rhythm. Hairline rules carry
  the structure, margins stay generous, density lives in the
  data and calm lives in the chrome.
- **Shape.** Square corners throughout. Photographs sit in
  thin ruled plates at one consistent crop. A drop with no
  verified photo gets a typographic absence plate, its
  initial set large in serif on paper, which reads as an
  intentional part of the system rather than a hole.
- **Imagery.** Real product photographs only, embedded from
  the official stores with provenance recorded per record in
  data/v1/drops.json. Never generated. Photo truth rule: a
  plate shows the exact series it labels, or it shows the
  absence plate.
- **Motion.** None, beyond a link underline. The river is a
  document. Reduced motion is therefore already honoured.
- **Chrome.** Wordmark plus three items: Archive, Real or
  Fake, Watchlist. A search field lives on the archive page
  itself. Current page is underlined in the chrome. Footer
  carries the full map and the counting rule.

## References, principle level only

- **Apple.com.** The navigation standard: almost nothing in
  the chrome, one idea per screen, product names set with
  total confidence, whitespace doing the persuading.
  Principle taken: restraint as authority. Layout not copied.
- **Land-book gallery standard (the fleet's primary
  benchmark).** Light winners run warm off whites, rationed
  colour and micro type density. Principle taken: one accent
  with a job, proof stated as numbers placed quietly.
- **BrickLink catalog.** For the Marvel ruling the user set
  BrickLink's substance as a model for a collector database.
  Principle taken: completeness and honest counts are the
  credibility of a catalog; every record carries its source.

## Recognizability test, set in advance

Cover the wordmark. A warm paper contents page ruled like a
book index, huge serif years down the left, one cherry red
reserved for dates and secrets, absence plates set like
initials in an old ledger. If that does not read as this
site alone, the build is not done.

## Retheme study, 2026-10-15 (user's direct order, supersedes the theme above)

The user, verbatim: "You need to match the theming of the
Sonny Angel community it's pink it's cute it's stationery
and artsy you know?" The cream and cherry editorial system
above is superseded as the THEME. The structure is not:
three link nav, one search, the chronological river, the
same three pages. Only the skin changes. His companion
orders the same night: all pictures load (photos are now
self hosted in this repo), copy cut down, present, do not
explain, and the home headline is just "Releases".

References, principle level only:

- **sonnyangel.com, the official Dreams site.** The brand's
  own world is pink: signature pink logo, sakura and heart
  motifs, soft product photography on pastel grounds, a
  dated news river for releases. Principle taken: pink is
  not decoration here, it is the brand's native ground, and
  the release river is already how Dreams itself presents.
- **Sanrio official online shop.** Character retail in
  pastel: product chips with rounded corners and gentle
  shadows, badges that read like stickers. Principle taken:
  the sticker treatment (white rim, soft shadow, rounded
  shape) is how this community's objects are presented.
- **Kawaii Pen Shop.** Stationery retail: soft color
  stories, washi tape and memo goods as the organizing
  objects, paper textures doing the framing. Principle
  taken: paper goods language, washi strips and stitched
  dashed lines as dividers and mounts, used sparingly.

Decisions:

- **Palette.** Soft pink paper #FFF8FA. Ink a warm plum
  #46303C. One deep pink #C2255C for everything that must
  be read (dates, secrets, links); a lighter pink #F783AC
  and blush #FDEBF3 carry the decoration. No gradients, no
  glow, no glass.
- **Type.** Rounded system sans (Arial Rounded, Hiragino
  Maru Gothic) for the wordmark, headlines, years and drop
  names. System sans for data. The serif is retired with
  the editorial theme.
- **Ornament, rationed to three.** A washi tape strip runs
  under the header and caps each photo plate, slightly
  rotated, with torn dashed edges. Rules are stitched:
  2px dashed in soft pink. Family labels and the eBay and
  store links are sticker chips, white rimmed, soft
  shadowed. Nothing else decorates.
- **Plates.** Rounded corners now. A photo with no verified
  source keeps the absence plate, its initial in light
  pink on blush.
- **Pills, noted.** Pill shapes are on the fleet blacklist
  as a generic tell. Here they are the theme's native
  grammar (stickers, tabs, tape), used only where the
  object is a sticker or a tab, never as a layout default.

Recognizability test, restated: cover the wordmark. A soft
pink sticker book page, washi taped photos, stitched
rules, big rounded years. If that does not read as the
Sonny Angel community's own scrapbook, the build is not
done.

## Blacklist v4, pre check

No purple or blue gradient dark theme. No glowing glass
cards. No card grid sameness; the river is rows, not cards.
No giant heading plus three cards rhythm. No pill
everything; corners are square. No filler copy; every line
on a page is data, a warning, or a direction.
